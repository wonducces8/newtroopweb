01 THE TROOP

Big skills.
Good people.

Troop 1941 is a close-knit, Scout-led community built around the things that matter: growing through challenge, looking out for one another, and giving back.

Chartered by the Isaak Walton League of America, we welcome families who want to explore Scouting together.
