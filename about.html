Troop 1941 helps young people grow into confident leaders through outdoor adventure, service, and learning by doing.

Rooted in our community

Troop 1941 is chartered by the Isaak Walton League of America and serves Scouts and families in the Leesburg area.

  • Scout-led patrols and programs
  • Outdoor skills and year-round adventures
  • Service projects close to home

Come see a meeting

We meet Mondays from 7:00–8:30 PM at Farmwell Station Middle School. Guests are welcome.

Enter through the cafeteria doors; the troop meets in the cafeteria. Bring your curiosity—uniform and handbook can come later.
